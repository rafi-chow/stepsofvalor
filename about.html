About the organization

Steps of Valor exists to support first responders and their families.

The stair climb is the current public event, but the organization’s broader mission is remembrance, resilience, community connection, and support for those who answer the call.

Mission

Honor, support, and serve.

Steps of Valor supports first responders and their families through programs, resources, community initiatives, and remembrance events that strengthen resilience and well-being.

The 2026 Steps of Valor 9/11 Memorial Stair Climb is the organization’s current public event.

Current event

Why the climb matters

The stair climb creates a respectful space to remember September 11, honor first responders, and bring the community together around service and sacrifice.

Community partner

Kappa Sigma Theta Omega is a community partner helping support the climb and service efforts. The organization is Steps of Valor.

Launch goal

The 2026 sponsorship packet and Spotfund fundraiser identify a $10,000 goal to help fund the mission of Steps of Valor.
