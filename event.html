Current event

2nd Annual Steps of Valor 9/11 Memorial Stair Climb

The stair climb is currently the public Steps of Valor event. Join the community at Maverick Stadium on September 11, 2026 for a morning of remembrance, movement, and support for first responders and their families.

What to expect

A respectful memorial stair climb shared with community members and first responders, with opening remarks, a moment of silence, and a coordinated 8:03 AM start.

What to bring

  • Comfortable athletic shoes
  • Personal water bottle
  • Weather-appropriate clothing
  • Anything you need to participate safely and respectfully

What to wear

Choose comfortable athletic clothing and shoes suitable for stairs. First responders and teams may follow their own group guidance.

Safety and weather

Climb with respect for your limits.

Participants should pace themselves, hydrate, and listen to event staff. Weather and safety updates will be shared before event day as needed.
